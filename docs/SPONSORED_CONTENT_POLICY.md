# Sponsored-content policy

SikshyaNepal may sell admission placements, profile promotion, or clearly labelled sponsored explainers. Payment never changes verification status, source requirements, editorial review, ranking outside the purchased placement, or removal of factual corrections.

Every paid item must display a clear `Sponsored` or `Paid placement` label near its title. It must link to an official source when it contains admission facts, and it must not promise admission, ranking, scholarships, outcomes, or government approval unless a primary source supports that claim.

Editors may reject, edit, pause, or remove a paid placement when its source is unavailable, its claim is misleading, its deadline has passed, or disclosure is missing. Sponsored status is recorded separately from verification status.
