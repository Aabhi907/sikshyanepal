# SikshyaNepal trust, safety and compliance checklist

Last engineering review: 10 September 2026

## Implemented in the product

- Visible keyboard focus and skip-to-content navigation.
- Descriptive text alternatives for content images; icon-only controls use accessible labels.
- Privacy, terms, cookies, copyright/takedown, community rules, child safety and refund pages.
- Optional advertising remains disabled until advertising consent is granted.
- OneSignal is loaded only after a user requests push notifications; browser permission is still required.
- Cookie settings can be reopened from the footer; rejecting optional services does not restrict public resources.
- Public forms explain why contact information is collected; high-risk forms require explicit acknowledgement.
- College reviews require authentication, remain pending until moderation and may include private verification evidence.
- Sponsored admissions and content require visible Sponsored/Paid labels.
- Automated high-risk claims (rankings, awards, results, salaries and “best college”) require human review and sources.
- Community content is pseudonymous publicly, privately accountable and pre-moderated.
- External links use safe new-tab attributes; PDF embeds use a descriptive title, lazy loading and no referrer.
- Operator location and role are disclosed without falsely claiming completed incorporation.

## Required owner actions before commercial launch

- Replace placeholder operational mailboxes with monitored addresses: info, safety, privacy, copyright and billing.
- After incorporation, add the exact legal company name, registration number, PAN/VAT number, registered address and official contact.
- Have a Nepal-qualified lawyer review policies, company objects, the community classification and advertising/refund terms.
- Configure Google consent requirements and privacy controls in AdSense as required for served regions.
- Document processors and contracts for Supabase, Vercel, Google, OneSignal and Resend.
- Create a written incident-response and lawful-request procedure, name the responsible officer and test it.
- Schedule deletion of expired community security events and document backup retention.
- Run accessibility and colour-contrast testing after every major visual release, including keyboard-only and screen-reader checks.
- Keep a licensed-source record for every marketing image, logo, video and externally republished asset.
- Do not launch payments until invoices, taxes, cancellation terms and the final refund process are operational.

This checklist supports engineering and operational readiness; it is not a substitute for legal advice.
